# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in Care101 welcoming, respectful, and safe for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

Because Care101 supports healthcare workflows, we also expect contributors to handle patient-related topics with care, professionalism, and respect for privacy.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Giving and receiving feedback professionally.
- Focusing on the work and its technical or user impact.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Protecting confidential information and never sharing real patient or personal data.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and working to correct them.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith argument.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing real patient records, credentials, payment details, or other sensitive data.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Scope

This policy applies to project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, chat spaces, events, and other project-related interactions. It applies when an individual is representing the project or materially affecting the project community.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this policy. They may remove or edit content, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical and will prioritize the safety and privacy of those involved.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact a project maintainer privately through the repository owner profile or another private channel listed by the maintainers. Do not include sensitive healthcare or personal data in a report.

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting publicly.
